## Drift-triggered diagnosis

Across the selected harmful-abrupt condition, 5 drift-triggered scored windows carried an active calibrated warning; the fraud-miss ADWIN alarmed in 5 such windows. The static-only row-error ADWIN baseline alarmed in 7 scored windows. The run-level total of applied challenger promotions was 5.
See `drift_triggered_diagnosis.csv` for each window’s fraud count, detector states, gate metrics, and event log. A warning is not itself evidence of successful adaptation; an unapplied or rejected candidate is reported as such.

False-alarm rates are measured on held-out no-drift seeds in the full run. If the run used `--quick`, the thresholds were calibrated on the same single seed and the false-alarm figures are only a smoke-test diagnostic.
